# F.R.I.D.A.Y. Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

**Last Updated: August 14, 2026**

## 1. Local-First Data Privacy
F.R.I.D.A.Y. is built with a **local-first architecture**. All conversation transcripts, meeting minutes, audio recordings, and learned habits are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database (`friday.db`).

## 2. End-to-End Encryption (E2E)
Cross-device synchronization uses industry-standard AES-256-GCM End-to-End encryption. The cryptographic master key is generated and held exclusively on your local device.

## 3. Privacy Control & Instant Data Purge
You maintain 100% control over your data:
- **7-Day Rolling Context**: Conversations older than 7 days are automatically pruned.
- **1-Click Memory Purge**: The Privacy Dashboard allows you to wipe all database records, OAuth tokens, and meeting transcripts instantly.

## 4. Biometric Authorization Gate
Sensitive actions (such as sending emails, dispatching SMS/Viber messages, or deleting records) require voice or biometric (Face ID / Touch ID / Windows Hello) confirmation.

## 5. Compliance & Data Residency
F.R.I.D.A.Y. complies with GDPR and CCPA regulations. Users can select cloud data residency regions (US, EU, Asia-Pacific).
